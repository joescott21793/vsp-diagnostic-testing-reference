Interpretation & report — required for diagnostic tests

For all diagnostic tests that require an interpretation and report, the medical record must include: clinical findings; interpretation of the findings, including comparative data referenced (when available); and the clinical management plan.

Copays

Copays, if required, apply to medical eye exams only (92002–92014, 99202–99205, 99211–99215, 99421–99423). Copays do not apply to non-exam services such as diagnostic testing, including fundus photography and OCT.
